Privacy Policy for PokéTicker: Pokémon Card Prices

Last updated: August 13, 2026

What this extension does

shows live Pokémon card market prices like a stock ticker, with a watchlist and trend arrows, in a toolbar popup.

Data collection

This extension does not collect any personal data. It has no accounts and no server. Nothing is sold or shared.

Local storage

Your watchlist (card names, ids, and last seen prices) is stored locally using the storage API. It never leaves your device and is cleared when you uninstall the extension.

Network

The popup requests the public Pokemon TCG API (api.pokemontcg.io) to search cards and fetch market prices. These requests include your IP address, as any web request does, and carry no personal data. The extension sends nothing else anywhere.
