Privacy Policy for Kaomoji Keyboard

Last updated: August 11, 2026

What this extension does

Kaomoji Keyboard is a popup of text faces and symbols you can browse, search, and copy with one click. It also keeps a list of your recent copies and lets you star favorites.

Data collection

This extension does not collect any personal data. It has no accounts, no login, and no network access of any kind. Everything it does happens locally in your browser.

What data is sent

Nothing. The extension never contacts a server. Your searches, recent copies, and favorites never leave your device.

Storage

The extension stores your favorites, recent copies, and chosen theme inside your browser profile on your own device using local storage. That data never leaves your browser and is cleared when you clear browser data or uninstall the extension.

Third parties

The extension includes no third party trackers, no advertising code, no analytics, and no remote scripts.

Your choices

You can remove the extension at any time through your browser settings. Removing it deletes its stored data from your profile.
