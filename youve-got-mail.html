Privacy Policy for Youve Got Mail

Last updated: August 11, 2026

What this extension does

Youve Got Mail watches your Gmail inbox for new messages from senders you choose and shows a classic notification and chime when one arrives. You can also set a plain timer mode that only counts down, with no Gmail access at all.

Gmail access

If you turn on the Gmail watch, the extension asks you to sign in with your Google account through Chrome's built in identity system. It then reads your Gmail inbox using the official Gmail API. This is a real network connection to Google: the extension requests a list of unread messages, and for the newest ones it reads only the From, Subject, and Date headers. It never reads message bodies, attachments, or conversation text.

What is collected and where it goes

Nothing about your mail is sent anywhere except Google, who already holds it. The extension has no server of its own. It only asks Google for the unread message list and the three headers above, and only for the senders you added. Everything else, like which senders you watch and your settings, stays in your browser's local storage on your own device.

Notifications and alarms

Chrome's alarm and notification systems are used so the watch can poll while the popup is closed and show the notification on your device. These run locally in your browser.

Removing access

You can stop the Gmail watch at any time from the popup, and you can revoke the extension's access from your Google account settings page. Your watched senders and settings are cleared when you clear browser data or uninstall the extension.
