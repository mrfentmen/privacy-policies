Privacy Policy for Winamp Player

Last updated: August 11, 2026

What this extension does

Winamp Player is a retro style music player in your browser popup. You add audio files from your own computer and play them locally.

Data collection

This extension does not collect any personal data. It has no accounts, no login, and no server. The audio files you add are read only on your device and are never uploaded anywhere.

Your playlist

Your playlist and settings are saved in your browser's local storage on your own device. They never leave your browser and are cleared when you clear browser data or uninstall the extension. Files you added in a previous session need to be picked again because the browser cannot keep access to local files between sessions.

Network

This extension makes no network requests of its own.
