Privacy Policy for YouTube Downloader

Last updated: August 11, 2026

What this extension does

YouTube Downloader saves public YouTube videos as MP4, MP3, or WAV files.

Paste a video link, check the video info, pick a format, and the file is saved

to the download location you have set in Chrome.

Data collection

This extension does not collect any personal data. It does not collect your

name, email address, location, web history, browsing activity, or any

identifying information. It has no accounts and no login.

What data is sent

When you paste a YouTube link, the extension sends that video ID to YouTube's

public oEmbed endpoint to fetch the title, author, thumbnail, and duration.

It then asks the open source Piped service for the public stream URL of that

video and downloads the stream through Piped's public proxy. This is the same

public data your browser already exchanges when you visit a video page, and

the requests contain no personal information.

If you choose a custom extraction instance in the advanced settings, the

extension asks you, in that moment, for permission to reach exactly that one

host. Nothing is sent to any other site.

Where processing happens

MP4 mode saves the video stream as it is. MP3 and WAV modes decode the video's

audio track with your browser's own audio engine and encode the file locally

using a bundled open source encoder. Your files and your download history

never leave your device.

Storage

The extension stores only a short list of your recent downloads and the

extraction instance you chose, inside your browser profile on your own device.

That data never leaves your browser and can be cleared whenever you clear your

browser data or uninstall the extension.

Third parties

The extension includes no third party trackers, no advertising code, no

analytics, and no remote scripts. The popup contains a clearly labeled,

non interactive reserved space that is not an advertisement and loads nothing.

Your choices

You can remove the extension at any time through your browser settings.

Removing it deletes its stored history from your profile.
