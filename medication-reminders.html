Privacy Policy for Medication Reminders

Last updated: August 11, 2026

What this extension does

Medication Reminders lets you list your medications and dose times. It shows real Chrome notifications when a dose is due and tracks your daily streak.

Data collection

This extension does not collect any personal data. It has no accounts, no login, and no server. Your medication list is stored only on your device.

Health information

Your medication names, doses, and schedule are sensitive health information. This extension keeps all of it in your browser's local storage on your own device. It is never sent to any server, never shared, and cleared when you clear browser data or uninstall the extension. Please be aware that browser storage is not encrypted, so treat this extension accordingly.

Notifications and alarms

The extension uses Chrome's notification and alarm systems so your reminders can fire even when the popup is closed. These run locally in your browser.
