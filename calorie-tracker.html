Privacy Policy for Calorie Tracker

Last updated: August 11, 2026

What this extension does

Calorie Tracker is a food log in your browser popup. You search a built in food list, log portions, and watch your daily calorie goal fill up.

Data collection

This extension does not collect any personal data. It has no accounts, no login, and no server. The food list is a built in local database and the app works fully offline.

Your log

Your food entries, meals, and goal are saved in your browser's local storage on your own device. They never leave your browser and are cleared when you clear browser data or uninstall the extension.

Network

This extension makes no network requests of its own.
