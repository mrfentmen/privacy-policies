Privacy Policy for Image to PDF

Last updated: August 11, 2026

What this extension does

a toolbar popup that combines images you add into a PDF file, with page reordering, page size, orientation, and margin options, then downloads the result.

Data collection

This extension does not collect any personal data. It has no accounts, no login, and no server. Everything it does happens locally on your device, and nothing is sold or shared.

Local storage

Nothing is stored. Images you add are held in memory only for the current popup session.

Network

This extension makes no network requests at all. Images are decoded and re-encoded locally on your device and never leave your computer.
