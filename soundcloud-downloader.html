Privacy Policy for SoundCloud Downloader

Last updated: August 11, 2026

What this extension does

SoundCloud Downloader turns public SoundCloud track and playlist links into

files you can keep. Paste a link, choose MP3 or WAV, and the file is saved to

the download location you have set in Chrome.

Data collection

This extension does not collect any personal data. It does not collect your

name, email address, location, web history, browsing activity, or any

identifying information. It has no accounts and no login.

What data is sent

When you paste a SoundCloud link, the extension sends that link to

SoundCloud's public website in the same way your browser already does when you

visit the track page. That request is needed to fetch the track metadata and

the public audio stream. No other website receives your link, and the request

contains no personal information.

For playlists, the extension may request each track in the set through the

same public SoundCloud interface. No data about you is included in those

requests.

Where processing happens

Everything else happens locally in your browser. MP3 mode saves the audio

SoundCloud serves to any regular visitor. WAV mode decodes that audio with

your browser's own audio engine and writes a standard 16 bit PCM file on your

machine. Your audio files and your download history never leave your device.

Storage

The extension stores only a short list of your recent downloads, inside your

browser profile on your own device, so the popup can offer one click repeats.

That data never leaves your browser and can be cleared whenever you clear your

browser data or uninstall the extension.

Third parties

The extension includes no third party trackers, no advertising code, no

analytics, and no remote scripts. The popup contains a clearly labeled,

non interactive reserved space that is not an advertisement and loads nothing.

Your choices

You can remove the extension at any time through your browser settings.

Removing it deletes its stored history from your profile.
